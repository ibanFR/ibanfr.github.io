title: Daily Standup
---

Reference for the Daily Standup structure, agenda, and desired outcomes.

## Overview

The Daily Standup is a recurring touchpoint (Mon–Fri) for the team to synchronise efforts and ensure consistent value
is delivered from the Sprint backlog. As a self-organising unit, this 15-minute session is the primary tool for
maintaining flow, identifying technical challenges early, and keeping delivery milestones on track.

## Agenda

### Yesterday's impact

Brief summary of completed tasks and their contribution to the current Sprint goal.

### Today's focus

Specific technical deliverables or milestones intended for the next 24 hours.

### Blocker identification

Immediate flagging of any environment issues, dependency delays, or technical debt preventing progress.

### WIP & flow check

A quick glance at the board to ensure work is moving and no developer is overloaded beyond our agreed WIP limits.

## Desired outcomes

- Unified team alignment on the highest priority tasks for the day.
- Identification and immediate escalation/assignment of all blockers.
- Maintenance of a healthy, predictable development cadence for our Sprint backlog.
