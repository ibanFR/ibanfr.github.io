## Technical Agile Coaching with the Samman method

Technical Agile Coaching with the Samman method provides a comprehensive guide to technical coaching in agile environments. It introduces the Samman method, which focuses on enhancing team performance through effective coaching techniques, fostering collaboration, and promoting continuous improvement.
