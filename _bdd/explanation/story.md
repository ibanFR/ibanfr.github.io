---
title: User Stories
---

A User Story is a short, simple description of a feature told from the perspective of the user or customer of the
system. It helps to capture the "who", "what", and "why" of a requirement in a way that is understandable to both
technical and non-technical stakeholders.

See Cucumber website for more details on [User Stories](https://cucumber.io/docs/terms/user-story).
