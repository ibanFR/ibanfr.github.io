> Refactoring is a disciplined technique for restructuring an existing body of code, altering its internal structure
> without changing its external behavior.
>
> <cite>Martin Fowler in his site [Refactoring](https://refactoring.com/)</cite>

## The Catalog

The [Refactoring Catalog](https://refactoring.com/catalog/) by Martin Fowler provides a comprehensive list of
refactoring techniques, each with a description, motivation, and example.
