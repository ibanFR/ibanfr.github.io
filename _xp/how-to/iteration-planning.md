This guide is meant to be a resource for anyone on the team who is facilitating a Sprint planning meeting. It's broken
down into two main sections:

- **Preparatory Activities:** Tasks to be done before the meeting.
- **Sprint Planning Activities:** The agenda for the meeting itself.

Some of the items in the guide may not be relevant to every sprint, so feel free to adapt it to your specific needs.
