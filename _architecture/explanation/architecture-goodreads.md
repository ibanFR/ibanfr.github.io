## Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software

Design Patterns by Erich Gamma, Richard Helm, Ralph Johnson, and John Vlissides is a seminal work on software design
patterns.

It introduces 23 classic patterns that provide proven solutions to common design problems, forming the foundation of
object-oriented design.
