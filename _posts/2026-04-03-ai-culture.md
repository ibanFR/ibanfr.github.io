While writing code is essential, the true hurdles in enhancing software delivery and operations performance are often
process friction and the speed of learning. This discussion explores AI as a powerful force multiplier for DORA
capabilities, extending its utility beyond simple code generation to include automated testing, documentation, and a
learning culture. We will shift the conversation from "Vibe Coding" toward establishing an "AI Culture"—one where AI
actively assists teams in pinpointing bottlenecks, lowering cognitive load, and fostering an environment dedicated to
continuous improvement. Could prioritizing a culture of continuous improvement through AI assistance significantly
bolster your organization's software delivery and operations performance?
